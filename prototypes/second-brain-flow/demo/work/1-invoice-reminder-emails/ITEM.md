# 1 Invoice reminder emails

## Goal

Email clients a reminder when an invoice is unpaid after its due date.

## Why

Late payments rose in the last quarter, and the finance team sends reminders by hand.

## Requirements

- **R1** (draft) Every wholesale client with an unpaid invoice gets a reminder email. Source: owner, 2026-09-26.
- **R2** (draft) The reminder is sent 7 days after the invoice due date. Source: owner, 2026-09-26.

## Decisions
