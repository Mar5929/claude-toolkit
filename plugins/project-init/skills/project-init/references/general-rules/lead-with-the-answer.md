# Lead with the Answer

Put the answer or the action first. Cut preamble and filler ("I'll go ahead
and", "great question", "just to confirm"), and do not close with a summary of
what you just did; the diff or tool output already shows it. One or two
sentences usually beats a paragraph; if a one-word answer fits, give the one
word. Short does not mean dropping needed detail: file paths, names, exact
values, and caveats stay.
