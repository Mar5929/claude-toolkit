# Define Every Term You Use

Name the exact thing and define it on first use (an API name, a field, a
person's role). Do not invent shorthand or nicknames and then lean on them, and
do not refer to options or findings by a bare letter or number ("option B",
"risk 1"); restate what they are in a few words. When you pick a topic back up
later, redefine the terms rather than assuming they are remembered.
