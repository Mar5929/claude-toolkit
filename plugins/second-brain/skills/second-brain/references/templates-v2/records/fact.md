# Replace this with the meaning, written as a statement

Replace this with one sentence that states the fact and nothing else.

## Detail

Replace this with the supporting explanation that shares this record's
evidence, truth status, and effective dates. A clause that would need
different evidence, a different truth status, or different dates belongs in
its own record.

Notes on the fields above:

- `epistemic_status` says how well the evidence supports this. Use
  `documented`, `observed`, `reported`, `diagnosed`, `approved`, `inferred`,
  `suspected`, or `unknown`. An `inferred` or `suspected` record must list the
  records it rests on in `based_on`.
- `evidence` needs at least one entry, and every entry needs a `source_type`
  and a `locator` someone else could follow.
- Another source for the same meaning is added to `evidence` here rather than
  becoming a second record.
