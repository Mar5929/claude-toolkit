## Section 3. Verdict

**Pass with fixes.**

Twenty-nine of the thirty-one rulings landed in full and the other two landed in part. Every ruling I could check against a primary source checks out: the `hooks.md` lines cited for the output cap, the timeout discard, the one-rule `if`, the scoped MCP name, the `UserPromptExpansion` path and the Stop continuation cap are all at the line numbers given; the `skills.md` sections "Inject dynamic context", "How injected commands run" and "When an injected command fails" exist with the content the design describes, as does the 1,536-character description cap; `plugins-reference.md` line 905 says what the design says it says; `memory.md` has "Enable or disable auto memory" with both the settings key and the environment variable; and requirement 14's twelve fields, requirement 16's eight PRD fields, requirement 17's three-clause Check and requirement 18's line 1307 all match the design's restatements. No ruling was reversed, and the three declined findings are declined for the reason the fix list gives.

The three most important remaining items:

1. **Fix the second startup hook's arithmetic** (finding 1). A forty-entry inbox, a full glossary and a full `current.md` come to about 9,600 characters against a 9,500-character budget, not the 7,700 the design states, so the one number the whole startup design rests on is still wrong after the round that was called to fix it. The section 10 seeded test asks for a pass that the stated sizes cannot give.
2. **Correct the two index rows in the parts table** (finding 2). They still promise up to 1,500 characters each at startup, which the print rule changed to a path and a count. The parts table is the document's record of what exists, and this row is also 2,880 characters of the budget in finding 1.
3. **Bring 13.13 and the compaction hold into line with the rulings** (findings 3 and 4). 13.13 names two Codex gaps the design no longer has, one of them the part ruling 4 deleted and one of them the behavior ruling B1 says is the same on both harnesses. `compact-hold.mjs` is the one hook with no timeout, against ruling B6's "every hook entry".
